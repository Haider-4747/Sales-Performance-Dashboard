## 📌 Business Questions & Insights
* **Revenue Overview:** Achieved total sales of **$1.86M** across **399 orders**.
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** Laptops dominate total sales volume, followed by Office Desks and Monitors.
* **Seasonal Trends:** Sales peaked in **March**, with steady performance across Q1 and Q2.
* **Channel Distribution:** Interactive slicing across Online, Corporate, and Retail channels.

---

## 🛠️ Data Pipeline & Methodology
* **ETL & Cleaning (Power Query):** Handled missing values, formatted temporal data (Year, Quarter, Month).
* **Data Modeling (Star Schema):** 1:N relationship between Products and Sales tables.
* **DAX Calculations:** Measures for `Total Sales`, `Orders Count`, and `Units Sold`.
